Tomer Weiss, Ph.D.

Assistant Professor, Informatics & Computer Science
New Jersey Institute of Technology
first.last [at] njit.edu / DBLP / CV / Research Statement

I am an Assistant Professor in the Informatics and Computer Science departments at the New Jersey Institute of Technology, where I have been on faculty since 2020. I completed my Ph.D. in Computer Science at UCLA, advised by Prof. Demetri Terzopoulos, and hold an M.S. from UCLA and a B.S. in Computer Science from Tel Aviv University.

My research develops real-time, scalable algorithms for simulating large virtual environments and the AI agents that populate them with a particular focus on crowd dynamics, physics-based multi-agent simulation, and the synthesis of 3D content. A running theme across my work is closing the gap between virtual and physical reality: making synthetic environments that behave, look, and feel like the real world. This has applications in many areas, from AR/VR, to generative AI.

Research Interests Real-time physics simulation / Multi-agent and crowd dynamics / Generative AI / Computer Vision / AR/VR/XR / 3D content creation

Opportunities

Existing NJIT undergrad or MS students in Computer Science or Informatics interested in collaborating must first enroll in IT 360. We do not have positions for students who have not completed this course. Project-based independent study opportunities (with or without academic credit) are available.

New Jersey high school students should have background in computer graphics — a game, visual application, shader, or similar — and proficiency in at least two of: Python, C++, C#, Java, Lua, or JavaScript.

Prospective PhD students should apply to the Information Systems PhD program at NJIT.

We currently have no positions for external applicants — students who have finished their BS, MS, or PhD outside NJIT and are seeking visiting, summer, or postdoctoral positions.
